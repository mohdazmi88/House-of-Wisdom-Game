Scholar's Field Guide

دَلِيلُ طَالِبِ الْعِلْمِ

1. Randomized Anti-Cheat System

Every scholar receives randomized question orders and rearranged answer choices (A, B, C, D). Comparing choice letters with peers will not match—you must comprehend the core concepts to succeed.

2. Timed Assessment Protocol

Each inquiry chamber allocates 60 seconds per question. If the timer reaches zero, the current selection is locked automatically. Stay swift, focused, and decisive.

3. Strict Linear Progression

The path of wisdom travels forward. You begin at Chamber 1 (Apprentice Tier) and must conquer each chamber sequentially. Once mastered, past chambers are cataloged as completed.

4. Boss Relic Mechanism Riddles

Completing milestone chambers activates historical 3D mechanism puzzles (such as calibrating the brass Astrolabe alidade or aligning the Camera Obscura pinhole). Solving them earns official downloadable relic certificates with your name and matric ID!

5. Final Survey & Database Submission

Click "Finish & Survey" at any time or upon conquering Chamber 15 to log your grades, answer the 5 research survey questions, and receive your comprehensive academic honours portfolio.
